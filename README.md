## Workflow Overview

### Project Goal

The goal of this project is to design an intelligent Agent for automatically producing and publishing content across Instagram, LinkedIn, Telegram, and Bale.

Content is generated based on each client's business domain, brand identity, defined writing tones, and approved topics.

In the first phase, the system will be developed for internal company use. In the future, it will be offered as a service to other companies and individuals.

### Overall Structure

The Workflow consists of two main parts:

- **Part A: Client Onboarding** — A one-time process for each client.
- **Part B: Content Production Pipeline** — A recurring process.

---

# Part A: Client Onboarding

## 1. Brand Profile

**Objective:** Collect the client's documentation and understand their business domain, policies, and target audience.

- **Inputs:** Documentation files (PDF and DOCX), the client's website, existing social media pages, and a structured questionnaire for clients who do not have sufficient documentation.
- **Structured Output:** The output of this stage must not be free-form text. It must be a profile with predefined fields, such as a JSON Schema.
- **Profile Fields:** Brand name, business domain, products and services, target audience (structured as personas), values and business policies, competitors, industry keywords, and content language.
- **Processing:** AI reads the documentation and populates the profile fields. Fields for which information is not available in the documentation must be marked accordingly. AI must not make assumptions or invent missing information.
- **Profile Completeness Assessment:**
  - Each field receives one of three statuses based on predefined criteria: Incomplete, Weak, or Complete.
  - Fields have different weights, and the overall score is calculated by code.
  - To pass this stage, all mandatory fields must be Complete, and the overall score must exceed a predefined Threshold.
  - The score is displayed to the client using a star-based rating system.
- **Missing Information Report:** If the profile does not meet the required Threshold, specific questions must be generated for each Weak field and presented to the client. This cycle continues until the Threshold is reached.
- **Storage:** Each client has an independent profile (Multi-tenancy), and profiles are versioned (Versioning).
- **Summary & RAG:** The profile summary must remain short and compact so it can be included in prompts used by subsequent stages. Original large documents must be stored separately and accessed through RAG when necessary.
- **Human in the Loop:** Human approval is not mandatory and is controlled through a Flag. Whenever human approval is required, the corresponding Flag is activated.
- **Cost:** Since an external Provider API is used, the implementation must remain simple and cost-efficient.
- **Open Items:** The exact Threshold and the details of the Human in the Loop process will be determined during the Production phase.

## 2. Tone & Style

**Objective:** The audience should feel as if multiple different administrators are managing the social media account. Content must also avoid repetition, as repetitive content can lead to audience loss.

- **Number of Writing Tones:** The client is asked how many different writing tones they want (for example, three tones). Each tone operates as a separate administrator.
- **Three-Layer Structure:**
  - **Identity Layer:** This layer is fixed and comes from the Brand Profile (for example, "You are a website designer").
  - **Tone or Administrator Layer:** The writing tones specified by the client are defined in a structured and compact format.
  - **Platform Layer:** The identity remains unchanged, but the writing style adapts to each platform. For example, Instagram content should be shorter because its audience may have less patience for lengthy content.
- **Tone Flexibility:** Each tone is defined as a personality rather than a set of rigid rules. Each personality has a short description, one or two sample texts, and a few main boundaries. This approach preserves room for creativity.
- **Repetition Prevention:** The latest posts generated by each administrator, along with their structures (opening style, format, and content angle), are recorded so AI can avoid repeating the same patterns.
- **Administrator Assignment:** Each post is assigned to an administrator based on the type of content.
- **Platforms:** All administrators are active across all platforms.
- **Storage:** Writing tones are part of the Brand Profile and are versioned (Versioning).
- **Human in the Loop:** Human approval is not mandatory and is controlled through a Flag.
- **Open Items:** The exact definition of each writing tone and the writing style for each platform will be determined after reviewing the client's documentation and policies.

## 3. Content Rules

**Objective:** Define permitted topics and the restrictions associated with each topic.

- **Whitelist:** Only approved topics are defined, and the system must work exclusively within these topics.
- **Topic-Specific Restrictions:** Each approved topic has its own restrictions. For example, within the Technology topic, discussing political issues or using certain writing styles may be prohibited.
- **Simplicity:** This stage is intentionally kept simple to avoid unnecessary costs and complexity.

# Part B: Content Production Pipeline

## 4. Source Collection

**Objective:** Collect relevant content from different sources based on the client's approved topics.

- **Source Management:** Define and manage the content sources used for each client.
- **Search Strategy:** Determine the topics and keywords to search for based on the Brand Profile and the client's Content Rules.
- **Extraction:** Extract content from the specified sources.
- **Search Fallback:** If the client has not provided a source Whitelist, Google Search is used to discover relevant sources and content.
- **Raw Content Schema:** Extracted content is stored in a structured format.
- **Scheduling & Storage:** Source collection is performed periodically, and the extracted information is stored.

## 5. Content Filtering & Selection

**Objective:** Evaluate the collected content and select items that are valuable enough to be transformed into social media content.

- **Duplicate Removal:** Identify and remove duplicate content.
- **Relevance Assessment:** Evaluate how relevant the collected content is to the Brand Profile and approved topics.
- **Freshness Assessment:** Evaluate publication dates and prevent old or outdated information from being selected.
- **Content Rules Validation:** Perform an initial validation of the content against the Whitelist and topic-specific restrictions defined in Stage 3.
- **Content Selection:** Rank the collected items and select the most suitable content for the content production stage.

**Output:** A collection of approved and selected content items ready to enter Stage 6.

## 6. Core Content & Image Generation

**Objective:** Transform selected sources into a centralized information package and generate an image that matches the content topic.

- **Administrator Selection:** Assign one of the predefined writing tones based on the content topic.
- **Content Brief Generation:** Extract and organize the main message, documented information, key points, and the content angle based on the selected sources.
- **Brand Identity Compliance:** Use the Brand Profile, the selected administrator's writing tone, and the client's Content Rules.
- **Source Attribution:** Preserve the connection between the generated content and its original sources. Prevent the addition of unsupported information.
- **Image Generation:** Generate an image appropriate for the content topic and subject.
- **Open Items Related to Image Generation:** The image generation method, the model or service to be used, and the visual style must be investigated and decided separately.

**Output:** A Content Brief accompanied by a generated image and information about the sources used.

## 7. Platform-Specific Content Generation

**Objective:** Generate independent content for each platform based on the centralized Content Brief created in Stage 6.

- **Inputs:** Centralized Content Brief, Brand Profile, administrator's writing tone, client's Content Rules, and platform-specific guidelines.
- **Independent Generation:** Separate content is generated for each platform.
- **Platform-Specific Formats:** Each platform's content structure and characteristics must be respected.
- **Content Diversity:** Each platform may approach the same topic from a different angle, depending on its audience and characteristics.
- **Information Accuracy:** All outputs must remain faithful to the information and sources provided in Stage 6.

**Content Strategy:** One topic, multiple independent pieces of content — not one text that is simply rewritten for different platforms.

**Output:** Platform-specific content ready for review.

## 8. Quality Control

**Objective:** Evaluate the quality of generated content before publication.

- **Automated Quality Control:** Evaluate factual accuracy, content quality, compliance with brand rules, and suitability for the target platform.
- **Human Approval:** The client must have the option to review and approve the generated content.
- **Trust Level:** As trust in the system increases, automatic publishing can be enabled for each client.

**Output:** Approved content ready for publication.

## 9. Scheduling & Publishing

**Objective:** Automatically publish content on the client's active platforms.

- **Scheduling:** Determine the publication time for each piece of content.
- **Publishing Intervals:** Maintain appropriate time intervals between consecutive posts.
- **Automatic Publishing:** Publish content through the platforms' APIs.
- **Platform Management:** Manage content publishing independently across Instagram, LinkedIn, Telegram, and Bale.

**Output:** Content published on the selected platforms.

## 10. Feedback & Analytics

**Objective:** Evaluate content performance and use the results to improve future content production.

- **Metrics Collection:** Collect performance data such as views, likes, comments, shares, and other engagement metrics.
- **Performance Analysis:** Analyze the performance of different topics, administrators, writing tones, and platforms.
- **Content Improvement:** Use the collected results to improve future topic selection, writing tones, and content types.

**Output:** Analytical data used for continuous improvement of the Workflow.

# General Design Principles

- Flexibility and the ability to expand the system in the future.
- Support for multiple clients (Multi-tenancy).
- Separation of each client's configurations and data.
- Consideration of AI API execution costs and keeping the architecture simple.
- The ability to enable or disable Human in the Loop approval through a Flag.
- Technical details of each stage will be decided when that stage enters the implementation phase.
